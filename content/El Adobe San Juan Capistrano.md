Today, Sun 29-Mar-2026, we went for Brunch/Lunch with some of our favorite people at the El Adobe in San Juan Capistrano.  It is a really fun restaurant in one of the oldest buildings in California, started in 1788. It has even been named a California state historical landmark.  
![[El Adobe de Capistrano.jpg]]

At one point we were surrounded by probably six or seven mariachis playing the greatest music.  I could hear every instrument individually and as a part of the whole. Such wonderful music and talented musicians.  All in all a wonderful day and experience.
