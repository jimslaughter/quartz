We will be flying from Los Angeles to Seattle on Alaska Airlines, then picking up a non-stop flight from Seattle to Reykjavik, Iceland.  For older folks, Reykjavik first came into our psyches in 1975 or so when President Ronald Reagan met Soviet Premier Gorbachev in Reykjavik. I always wondered why they picked Reykjavik, but take a look at a map it it becomes clear instantly. It's a straight shot from both America and Russia which is pretty close to half way between the two then superpowers.  

You may be wondering why and how we decided to go to Iceland for a vacation.  It all started last summer, when my siblings Noreen, Don and Sheila were discussing where might be a fun next place to go on cruise together.  In May of 2024 we four and our spouses boarded a cruise ship in Montreal for a weeklong cruise up the St. Lawrence River, through the Canadian provinces of Quebec, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, and Nova Scotia, then down to Boston.  That was a really fun trip.  As I understand it (I wasn't there), Sheila (or maybe it was Noreen) threw out the question "Where shall we go next?".  And it was Don, according to the story, who said "How about Iceland?" And they all said that sounded great.  Sheila said she wanted to try out Viking Cruise Lines (which is a bit of a departure for our family because the only cruise line we'd ever gone on as Holland America.) So that's how we ended up going to Iceland.  If we have a terrible time there we can blame Don.  

We'll be in Reykjavik for three days, then we'll start our trip around the island of Iceland.  We've got a few local tours scheduled before we set sail.
