Our first port of call will be Ísafjorour, which is a town in the Westfjords region of 
northwest Iceland. It's known for its dramatic landscapes. It sits at the top of a long inlet that I guess would be called a fjord in Icelandic.  There are many picturesque houses with
corrugated tin roofs built by fishing merchants in the 18th and 19th centuries. 

There is a museam that shows the town's maritime history, including a collection of old
fishing boats. The old hospital, now a cultural center, contains archives and photography.
